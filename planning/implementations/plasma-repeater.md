# Implementation — Plasma Repeater, Reach repeater bolt (2026-10-09)

Seventh weapon of the weapons pass. `covenant_remnants_plasmarepeater` (small energy EMP bolt, the
fighter gun: 7 mounts on the three wing variants, no ship slots) turns from a vanilla Ion Cannon
mirror (vanilla `ioncannon_shot`, vanilla sound) into the **Halo: Reach plasma repeater bolt**: a
blue-violet teardrop with a hot head, the Reach repeater shot, and a slightly faster refire. The
Ion Cannon's EMP arcs stay.

## Developer rulings (2026-10-09)

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Look | Reach plasma repeater bolts: short teardrop, hot core, blue fringe + glow, small hit glow. |

| Mount art | deferred: vanilla `ion_cannon_*` sprites, violet glow. |

Vanilla anchor (0.98a-RC8): Ion Cannon 25 + 400 EMP, 0.5 s, 500 range, 30 flux, 6 OP.
