Eighth weapon of the weapons pass. `covenant_remnants_plasmaautocannon` (large energy burst bolt,
7 mounts on 4 variants) turns from a vanilla Autopulse Laser mirror (vanilla `autopulse_shot`,
vanilla sound) into the **Halo: Reach Seraph quad cannon bolt**: a long cyan-blue streak with a hot
head, the Seraph cannon shot, and +100 range.

## Engine note — where the bullet sprite sits

`BALLISTIC_AS_BEAM` (obf `combat.entities.MovingRay.render` → `renderers.N`): the glow is drawn at
the ray head (`to`, the true position); the bullet sprite is drawn from `to` **forward** by
(sprite height / sprite width) × `width`; the tracer runs from that forward tip (alpha 0) through
`to` (full) back to `from` (alpha 0), `from`→`to` = `length`. So a long sprite pushes the visible
head ahead of the hit point. The Pulse Laser (12×48, width 5: 20 su ahead) and Plasma Repeater
(12×40, width 9: 30 su ahead) have the same offset; both were signed off as they are.
